# Salem Fellowship Church (SFC) — Hyderabad

A lightweight, modern, multi-page web and mobile application crafted specifically for **Salem Fellowship Church (SFC)** in Hyderabad, Telangana. Built with **React 18 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS + React Router v7**, with warm sacred colors, sharp edges, and zero heavy bloat.

---

## 🏛 Church Identity & Specific Details

* **Church Name**: **Salem Fellowship Church (SFC)**
* **Ministry Type**: Local community fellowship with 6 branches across Hyderabad.
* **Language**: **All sermons and worship services are in Telugu** (తెలుగు పరిచర్య).
* **Scripture Verse**: *“ఎక్కడ ఇద్దరు లేక ముగ్గురు నా నామమున కూడియుందురో అక్కడ నేను వారి మధ్యన ఉందును.”* — మత్తయి (Matthew) 18:20

## ⏰ Liturgical Rhythm

1. **Sunday Divine Service (All Branches)**:
   * **10:00 AM – 01:00 PM (3 hours)** in **Telugu**. Songs, praise & adoration, expository preaching, and communion.
2. **Tuesday In-Depth Bible Study (Kalyan Nagar Main Branch)**:
   * **Every Tuesday from 06:00 PM to 07:00 PM** in **Telugu**. Verse-by-verse scripture study.
3. **Monthly All-Night Prayer (All Branches)**:
   * **Every Last Saturday of the month from 10:00 PM to 05:00 AM** at all church branches.

## 📱 Features & Pages

* **[HomePage](file:///c:/Users/John%20Emmanuel%20Martin/Documents/VSCode_Work/sfc_work/src/pages/HomePage.tsx)** (`/`): Service announcements, 6 quick actions, Telugu broadcast spotlight, Hyderabad branches grid, Tuesday Bible study callout, and monthly all-night prayer banner.
* **[LivePage](file:///c:/Users/John%20Emmanuel%20Martin/Documents/VSCode_Work/sfc_work/src/pages/LivePage.tsx)** (`/live`): Dedicated YouTube player for the Sunday 10 AM – 1 PM livestream, categorized Telugu archive (*Sermons*, *Bible Study*, *Telugu Worship*, *All-Night Prayer*, *Youth*), and search.
* **[BranchesPage](file:///c:/Users/John%20Emmanuel%20Martin/Documents/VSCode_Work/sfc_work/src/pages/BranchesPage.tsx)** (`/branches`): Filter by locality (Kalyan Nagar, Alwyn Colony, Vanasthalipuram, Gachibowli, Paatigadda, Prem Nagar), full service timetables, pastor phone call, WhatsApp, and Google Maps links.
* **[EventsPage](file:///c:/Users/John%20Emmanuel%20Martin/Documents/VSCode_Work/sfc_work/src/pages/EventsPage.tsx)** (`/events`): 3 pillars timetable (Sunday 10-1, Tuesday 6-7 PM, Last Sat 10 PM - 5 AM) and downloadable `.ICS` calendar reminders.
* **[GivingPage](file:///c:/Users/John%20Emmanuel%20Martin/Documents/VSCode_Work/sfc_work/src/pages/GivingPage.tsx)** (`/give`): UPI QR Code, copyable VPA (`salemfellowship@okhdfcbank`), mobile 1-tap app launch (`upi://pay`), Bank NEFT/RTGS details, and Section 80G tax receipt request form.
* **[PrayerPage](file:///c:/Users/John%20Emmanuel%20Martin/Documents/VSCode_Work/sfc_work/src/pages/PrayerPage.tsx)** (`/prayer`): Confidential Telugu prayer petition intake, selecting branch in Hyderabad, with 24/7 hotline and WhatsApp links.
